# Godthegarden

Personal technical blog documenting my learning, projects, and experiences in cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and software development.

**Website:** https://godthegarden.github.io/

## About

I'm Jeong Won Shin, an undergraduate student majoring in AI and Cybersecurity at Korea University Sejong Campus.

This blog serves as a space to document what I learn, explore technical topics, and share experiences from projects and activities.

## Topics

- **Development** — Programming and software development
- **CTF & Wargame** — Security challenges and write-ups
- **Bug Bounty** — Vulnerability research and security testing
- **Technical Writing** — Technical notes and documentation
- **Research & Conferences** — Research papers and conference insights
- **Activities** — Hackathons, competitions, certifications, and other experiences
